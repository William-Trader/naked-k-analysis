# Pattern catalog (Al Brooks)

> Every entry is a **candidate**, not a certainty. Context (market cycle,
> always-in, location) must confirm it. Quality is 0-100; the arbitration
> layer promotes at most one primary and one secondary pattern.

| Pattern | Type | Prerequisites | Trigger | Invalidation | Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DB | Reversal | Two swing lows within 0.8%; price above the midpoint | Close above the neckline | Close below the lower low | Neckline + (neckline - low) |
| DT | Reversal | Two swing highs within 0.8%; price below the midpoint | Close below the neckline | Close above the higher high | Neckline - (high - neckline) |
| HS | Reversal | Three peaks, middle highest, shoulders similar | Close below the neckline | Close above the right shoulder | Neckline - (head - neckline) |
| IHS | Reversal | Three troughs, middle lowest, shoulders similar | Close above the neckline | Close below the right shoulder | Neckline + (neckline - head) |
| Wedge top | Reversal | Three rising highs and lows, narrowing | Trendline break + bearish signal bar | Above the third high | Wedge start / measured move |
| Wedge bottom | Reversal | Three falling highs and lows, narrowing | Trendline break + bullish signal bar | Below the third low | Wedge start / measured move |
| H2 | Trend | Bull structure; second higher low | Buy stop above the signal bar high | Below the H2 low | Prior high / measured move |
| L2 | Trend | Bear structure; second lower high | Sell stop below the signal bar low | Above the L2 high | Prior low / measured move |
| Final flag | Reversal | Late trend; tight 8-15 bar range | Failed breakout of the flag | Beyond the flag extreme | Flag start |
| BO PB | Trend | Strong breakout; pullback to the breakout level | Same-direction signal bar | Back inside the prior range | Measured move |
| EMA20 PB | Trend | Strong trend; pullback to EMA20 | Close back in trend direction | Beyond the EMA20 swing | Prior high/low |
| MTR | Reversal | Trendline break + HL/LH + strong reversal bar | Break of the micro trendline | Beyond the prior swing | Prior leg / measured move |
| Triangle | Continuation | Converging highs/lows (asc/desc/sym) | Breakout with follow-through | Back inside the triangle | Triangle height |
| Cup and handle | Reversal | U-shaped base + small right-side pullback | Break above the cup rim | Below the handle low | Cup depth |
| Climax | Exhaustion | Range > 2x ATR and volume > 2.5x average | Reversal bar / failed follow-through | Beyond the climax extreme | Prior swing / 2 legs |
| TR | Range | 20+ overlapping bars; ADX < 25 | Edge rejection or confirmed breakout | Beyond the range edge | Opposite edge |
